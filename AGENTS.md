This repo holds three separate projects; keep their code apart. `cifar10/` is the DDPM on CIFAR-10 diffusion work (runs on the Windows RTX 5090 PC). `mnist/` is the small MNIST bit-flip study (runs on the MacBook): before working on it, read [`mnist/plan-mnist-week1.md`](mnist/plan-mnist-week1.md) and add a work-log row after every step. `resnet20/` is the Week 2 ResNet-20 bit-flip study on Fashion-MNIST and CIFAR-10 (runs on the Windows RTX 5090 PC): before working on it, read [`resnet20/plan-resnet20-week2.md`](resnet20/plan-resnet20-week2.md) and add a work-log row after every step.
